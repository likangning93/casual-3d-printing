#### 0002 Debrief ####

Things I don't like:
* limited to one rev before rewinding, due to the thickness of the winding beam
* still very fast and angry due to torque
* assembly was extremely annoying, sliding things along the pencil shafts from one side

Next steps:
* try again with twisting the rubber band instead of pulling it
* benefits:
  - elastic potential spread across a lot more revolutions
  - winding would be in the power delivery direction
  - so, only need a single one-way-gear on the winding end
  - and so the winding end stays fixed when running
* might need a reduction gear to produce enough torque, 1-4 might be a good start?
* pair with a matched multiplier gear for winding would help reduce annoyance of winding
* for body assembly, sandwich the pencils between beams with rectangular cutouts and wrap with tape
* don't need bottom two pencils at all, just prop it up on some feet
